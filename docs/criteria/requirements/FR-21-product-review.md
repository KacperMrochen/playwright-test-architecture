# FR-21 — Write a product review

The site accepts a review on a product's detail page.

Site reference: TC21.

## Acceptance criteria

- AC-21.1 Given `/product_details/<id>` under "Write Your Review", when a
  name, email and review are submitted, then "Thank you for your review."
  appears. No login is required, and the review isn't shown back on the
  page.
