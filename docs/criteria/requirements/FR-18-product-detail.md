# FR-18 — The product detail page describes the product

The site shows a product's full information on its own page.

Site reference: TC08.

## Acceptance criteria

- AC-18.1 Given `/product_details/<id>`, then the page shows the product's
  name, category as "Category: <Usertype> > <Category>", price as
  `Rs. <number>`, availability, condition and brand, plus a quantity field
  and an "Add to cart" button.
